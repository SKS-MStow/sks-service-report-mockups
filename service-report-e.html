No microphone audio in Boardroom 3.01

Flinders Medical Research · Sarah Nguyen 1 King William St, Adelaide SA 5000 Level 3 · Boardroom 3.01 Cisco Room Kit Pro · Ceiling Mic Pro Tech Mark Stow 1.5 hrs on site

The problem

Far-end participants couldn’t hear anyone in the room during Teams and Webex calls. Local playback worked, but the ceiling microphones weren’t being picked up by the codec.

→

What we fixed

The Ceiling Mic Pro had dropped off the network after a recent switch firmware update turned off PoE on its port. We re-enabled PoE, replaced a damaged patch lead at the wall plate, re-paired the mic to the codec and ran a test call with your IT team — audio is clear in both directions.

Gear used

1×CAT6A patch lead, 2 mReplaced damaged lead at wall plate1×RJ45 keystone insertWall plate re-terminated

Our advice going forward

  • Let us know before any network switch firmware updates — AV ports need PoE locked on.
  • Run a quick test call after network changes so issues are caught before a meeting.
  • Keep a spare patch lead in the credenza for a fast swap if it happens again.
